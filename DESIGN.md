# FuniqLab design

The film occupies a fixed viewport. Project titles and short transmissions follow the camera through the journey. A sparse binary rain builds the opening scene. Captions resolve into readable text as each chapter enters.

## Typography and material

English headings use Orbitron and body text uses IBM Plex Mono. Chinese headings use Glow Sans SC Wide and body text uses Glow Sans SC Condensed. The panels use a neutral grey glass surface, thin engraved arcs and a rotating brushed-metal emblem. Light on the emblem moves independently of the grain.

## Navigation

The right semicircle maps film time to a continuous arc. Elapsed sections and reached stations fill as the visitor scrolls. The left semicircle presents a rotating project list. Only the centered project displays its description and opens a link. The toolbar and scroll hint have dedicated space on small screens.

## Motion

Scroll position determines film time in both directions. The Earth frame holds through the end of the journey. Reduced-motion mode uses still images and static typography.
